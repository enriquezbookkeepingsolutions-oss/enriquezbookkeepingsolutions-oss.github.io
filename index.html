FAQ

Questions before you book?

What bookkeeping services do you provide?

EBS offers monthly bookkeeping, account reconciliation, invoicing and accounts receivable support, financial reporting, bookkeeping cleanup, and seasonal straightforward individual tax preparation.

Do you work with businesses virtually?

Yes. Virtual bookkeeping services are available, allowing EBS to support businesses beyond the immediate local area.

Do I need to use QuickBooks Online?

QuickBooks Online is a primary platform supported by EBS. During your consultation, we can discuss your current bookkeeping setup and whether QuickBooks Online is a good fit.

Can you help if my books are behind or disorganized?

Yes. Bookkeeping cleanup is available for businesses that need help getting records organized before moving into ongoing monthly bookkeeping support.

Do you prepare tax returns?

Seasonally, EBS offers preparation of straightforward individual federal tax returns, primarily W-2 income and Form 1040 returns. More complex tax situations may require a different tax professional.

What happens during the free consultation?

The 20-minute consultation is a conversation about your business, your bookkeeping process, your challenges, and the type of support you may need. There is no obligation to purchase services.
